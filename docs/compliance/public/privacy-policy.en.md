# Privacy Notice for Parents — Wondimo

*Last updated: [DATE]*

This notice explains in plain language what data we process when your child uses Wondimo, why we process it, where it is stored and how you stay in control. There is also a **version for children** ([link]) you can read together.

## At a glance
- **Only you have an account.** Your children use *profiles* inside your account: a nickname, an avatar picked from a list, and their school year or age band. We **do not ask** for a child's date of birth, surname, photo, email or phone number.
- **No ads, no trackers, no selling of data.** We only use the technical cookies the service needs to work.
- **You can read every chat** your child has with the AI assistant. Chats are **automatically deleted after 90 days**, and you can delete them sooner.
- **Voice is not recorded or kept.** We use it only to turn speech into text, then delete it straight away.
- **Data stays in Europe** (servers in Frankfurt). [⚠️ DA VERIFICARE: true only with the OpenRouter EU endpoint or the direct Mistral API; some providers are US companies, see section 7]
- **We do not use your family's data to train AI.**

## 3. How the AI assistant works
- It is a **computer program, not a person**, and it always says so to your child.
- It runs on open-weight language models from **Mistral AI** (a French company), with safety rules tailored to each age band.
- **By default it guides your child to reason things out** instead of giving the answer straight away. You can make it more direct.
- Every message, in and out, goes through automatic moderation.
- **It can make mistakes.** We teach it to say "let's check with a grown-up".
- **It does not make decisions** about your child. Game levels are **suggestions for you** only.
- Our AI providers run in **zero data retention** mode: they process the request and keep neither the text nor the voice.

## 4. Sensitive topics and alerts
If your child brings up topics such as bullying, abuse, self-harm or danger, the assistant will:
1. reply gently and encourage your child to talk straight away with a trusted adult (you, a teacher or another grown-up), and give a free children's helpline (e.g. Childline 0800 1111 in the UK, 147 in Switzerland, 19696 in Italy) [⚠️ DA VERIFICARE numbers];
2. not go into the topic in depth;
3. **alert you straight away** in the parent area, and send an email with no sensitive details in it.

We do not report anything to third parties automatically. [⚠️ DA VERIFICARE procedure for illegal content]

## 8. Your rights (and your child's)
For yourself and your child, you can:
- **see** all the data (parent area) and **download** it (portability);
- **correct** it;
- **delete** a conversation, a profile or the whole account, directly in the parent area;
- **withdraw consent** at any time (the AI assistant is then switched off for that profile);
- **object** to usage statistics;
- **restrict** processing;
- **complain** to a supervisory authority: the Italian Garante (www.garanteprivacy.it), the authority in your EU country, the UK ICO (ico.org.uk) or the Swiss FDPIC (edoeb.admin.ch).

We reply within one month. Write to [EMAIL PRIVACY] from your account email address.

## 9. Security
We use encryption in transit and at rest, strict separation between families, a PIN for the parent area, multi-factor authentication for administration, and safety testing of the AI assistant before every release. No system is 100% secure. If something happens that puts your child's data at risk, we will tell you.

## 10. Cookies
We only use **technical** cookies and browser storage, needed to keep you signed in and to remember language and active profile. No profiling or third-party analytics cookies, so there is no consent banner.
