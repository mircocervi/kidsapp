# How [NOME APP] works, explained for you!

Hi! This page tells you what happens when you play and talk with [NOME APP]. Read it with your mum, dad or a grown-up you trust.

## 🤖 Your mascot is a robot program
Your mascot is a **computer program**. It's called "artificial intelligence" (AI).
- **It is not a person.** It doesn't have a body and doesn't live anywhere.
- It's good at answering questions, but **sometimes it gets things wrong**. If something seems odd, ask a grown-up!
- It will often ask you questions to help you **work out the answer yourself**. That's how your brain gets stronger! 💪

## 📝 What we know about you
We only know a few things:
- the **nickname** you picked (you don't need your real name);
- the **avatar** and **mascot** you picked;
- what **school year** you're in, so the games are right for you;
- which **games** you played and what you asked your mascot.

We **don't** know where you live, your surname or what you look like. And we don't want to know!

## 🙊 Keep your personal stuff private
**Don't type or say** to your mascot:
- your full name,
- your address or your school,
- phone numbers or passwords.

## 👀 Who can read your chats
**Your mum, dad (or the grown-up who looks after you) can read** your chats with your mascot. They do it to help you and to know you're OK.
After a while (about **3 months**) the chats **disappear** by themselves.

## 🎤 When you talk
If you talk to your mascot, your voice is only used to understand what you said. Then we **throw it away straight away**. We don't keep it.

## 💛 If something makes you feel bad
If someone hurts you, or you feel sad or scared:
- **tell a grown-up you trust straight away**: a parent, a teacher, a grandparent, an aunt or uncle;
- you can also call a children's helpline **for free**: in the UK, **Childline 0800 1111**; in Switzerland, **147**. [⚠️ DA VERIFICARE: your mascot will show you the right number for your country]
- If you are in danger **right now**, call **999** (UK) or **112**.

Your mascot will tell you the same things and **let your parents know** so they can help you. It isn't spying: it's because **you matter**.

## 🗑️ You can delete everything
Your parents can delete your chats or your profile whenever they want. If you want something deleted, ask them.

## ❓ Questions?
Ask your parents. They can write to us.
